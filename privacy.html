GMRS Contacts Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026. Developer: WSFU624. Privacy contact: wsfu624@gmail.com.

This policy describes the data practices of GMRS Contacts (GMRS Contacts Logbook, Android package com.gmrscontacts.app), version 1.0. The app helps radio users maintain contact and repeater logs and display radio-related locations. The app's My Account feature is a local license profile rather than a developer-operated online account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, look up or save, including callsigns, names, mailing addresses, optional phone numbers and email addresses, communication history, frequencies, repeater details, notes, license information, home station information, map coordinate caches, preferences and optional map API credentials. It uses these records to provide logging, lookup, mapping, reminders and backup features.

Records and preferences are stored in the app's local database and Android app storage. The developer does not operate a central account or contact database for this app. Android or your device's backup services may retain copies depending on your settings.

Network requests and third-party services

  • Callsign and license lookups send the requested callsign to services such as OpenCallbook and Skywave. Results may be based on publicly available FCC license records. FCC data may also be used for license lookups.
  • When address lookup is enabled, saved addresses, including a home station address when applicable, may be sent to Android's geocoding service to determine map coordinates. Coordinates may be cached on your device.
  • With your permission, the app uses device location while the map is active to display your position and calculate distances and bearings. Requests to map services such as OpenStreetMap and the selected ArcGIS imagery provider can reveal the displayed geographic area, IP address and network metadata. ArcGIS imagery may use an API key that you provide.
  • Software update checks request release information from GitHub. The current app opens downloads in your browser. Google Play distribution and updates are also subject to Google's privacy practices.
  • External repeater searches, FCC services, and other links open third-party websites or apps. Those services handle your data according to their own privacy policies. Phone and email links open your chosen dialer or email app; GMRS Contacts does not send messages or make calls itself.
  • When you choose a repeater screenshot, the app accesses that selected image for text recognition using Google ML Kit. The app uses the recognized information to assist with repeater entry. SDKs and network services may process technical or diagnostic information under their own policies.

Third-party services may receive the information needed to fulfill a request, including queries, location-related map requests, IP addresses and other network metadata. Their own privacy and retention practices apply. For further information, see Google's Privacy Policy, OpenStreetMap Foundation's Privacy Policy, Esri's privacy information and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Permissions, backups and security

Location access is optional and can be revoked in Android settings. The app requests notification permission for license expiration reminders. Android's file and image pickers provide access to files you choose. Import and export features read and write user-selected backup files; these can include personal information, such as contact or home addresses. Exported JSON backups are not encrypted by the app. Store them in a trusted location and do not share them with unauthorized people.

The app uses Android's application storage controls and HTTPS for the directly configured web lookup and update endpoints. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device, optional map API credentials and backups.

Advertising and support

Version 1.0 does not include an advertising SDK or in-app purchase system. If advertising or other monetization features are introduced, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated to reflect the data practices of those features before their release.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Locally saved data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. You may delete saved contacts and repeaters using their respective controls. You can remove local profile data and cached information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings; this also removes other local app data. Hiding a home marker or disabling location access does not erase previously cached information. Android backups and files you exported may remain until you delete them separately. The developer cannot erase third-party service records or user-controlled backups on your behalf.

If you contact the developer voluntarily, the correspondence and information you choose to provide may be retained as needed to respond to your request and meet applicable obligations. Contact the privacy email above to ask about or request deletion of information you supplied directly. Do not send sensitive license records, credentials or other people's personal information unless necessary and authorized.
